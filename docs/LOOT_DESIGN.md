# Loot Design

Mob chests contain randomized vanilla Minecraft rewards.

- Common mobs: basic resources and a low chance of iron/golden gear.
- Mid-tier mobs: better resources, enchanted books, and occasional diamond gear.
- Strong mobs: stronger enchanted diamond gear and higher rarity rolls.
- Bosses and Warden: a low-probability Netherite-tier reward, with no guaranteed OP drop.

All custom items, custom enchantment books, Golden Food, and Liquid Gold rewards are removed.
