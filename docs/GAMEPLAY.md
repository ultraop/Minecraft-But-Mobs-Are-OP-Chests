# Gameplay

1. Mob starts with normal vanilla behavior.
2. First right-click freezes it in place; it does not sit, tame or become a pet.
3. Second right-click opens its body at the middle seam: upper part rotates as a chest lid; lower part stays as the base.
4. One loot GUI session is allowed per mob.
5. After the session, the chest can never open again.
6. The mob returns to normal behavior.
7. Player may kill it; normal vanilla drops and XP remain enabled.

Generate and persist loot once. GUI close consumes the opening; discard uncollected items. No reroll by relogging, chunk unload, reopening, ownership change or restart. Prevent simultaneous sessions. Special chest loot is never dropped again on death.

Entity state and loot are global to the mob. Legend progress is per player.
