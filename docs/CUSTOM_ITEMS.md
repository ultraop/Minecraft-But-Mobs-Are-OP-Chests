| Infernal Edge | Controlled flame strike |

Custom enchantments: Skybound, Featherstep, Swiftwind, Vitality Pulse, Flameheart, Thunderstrike, Deepwalker, Miner's Instinct, Echo Sight and Berserker.

Custom level 30–60 is an independent rank, not vanilla Sharpness/Protection. Apply hard caps, cooldowns and duration limits. Prevent unlimited stacking. Rare gear should not yield a full armour set from one chest.
